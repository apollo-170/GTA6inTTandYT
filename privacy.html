ClipForge — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026

This policy explains the current data handling of the ClipForge Windows desktop application, a local tool for stream monitoring, video editing, and optional posting to connected social media accounts.

1. Information used by ClipForge

When you connect an account, ClipForge may process the account identifier, display name, authorization access and refresh tokens, and granted permissions needed to perform actions you select. Locally, it may keep application settings, connected channels, stream event metadata, clip records, and paths to source and exported video files.

2. Where the data is stored

ClipForge processes recordings and creates exports on your computer. For its native YouTube and TikTok integrations, credentials are stored using Windows Credential Manager. Application settings and clip metadata are stored locally. Disconnecting an account in ClipForge removes the application's stored connection where supported; you can also revoke access from the connected platform's account settings. Removing the application does not necessarily remove every locally generated file or Windows credential.

3. When information is sent to other services

When you sign in or publish a video, the application communicates with the applicable platform, such as TikTok, YouTube, or Twitch, using the platform's APIs. Video uploads, account information requests, and authorization exchanges are processed by those providers under their own privacy policies. If you choose an optional hosted publishing integration or configured AI vision endpoint, relevant account, video, or selected-frame data may be sent to that provider when you use that feature. Do not enable such integrations unless you understand the provider's policies.

4. Local operation and permissions

The ClipForge project does not require a ClipForge-operated cloud account for its native desktop editing workflow. You choose which accounts to connect and which videos to publish. The application needs an internet connection for OAuth, online monitoring, uploads, and update checks.

5. Data retention and your choices

You control source recordings and exported videos stored on your computer and can delete them directly. You can disconnect social media accounts in ClipForge and revoke platform access through the platform. Connected services may retain information according to their own policies. ClipForge has no ability to delete data already published on third-party platforms on your behalf through these local controls.

6. Changes and contact

This policy may change as ClipForge features and connected services evolve. The effective date will be updated. For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through ClipForge GitHub Issues. Do not post passwords, tokens, or sensitive personal information in public issues.
